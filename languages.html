Languages

Language learning has been my favorite hobby for over a decade now, starting with French and progressing to Spanish, Latin, Ancient Greek, German, Hindi, and most recently Chinese (to varying degrees of proficiency). In high school, I competed and won first place in both the North American Computational Linguistics Olympiad and the International Linguistics Olympiad.

Chinese

Ever since I started the Duolingo Chinese course in 2023, the Chinese language has been one of my life's great passions. In March 2026, I passed level 6 of the rigorous HSK exam, indicating advanced proficiency, after three years of self-study and regular online lessons. During the same time period, I have studied Chinese calligraphy at Carnegie Mellon; learned a bit of Classical Chinese; and traveled to China, particularly the southwest, several times. If you speak Chinese, are interested in learning, or want to practice, I would love to hear from you!

Ranch

Two childhood friends and I bought 10 acres of undeveloped land in southern Colorado, christening it Riddlers Ranch, and built a small cabin there entirely from scratch with no hired labor. Through this process, we learned a ton about everything from electrical wiring to stucco application to how to properly seal saltillo tile, mostly from YouTube videos. It was definitely more work than we had bargained for originally, but worth it to share with friends.
