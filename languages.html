Languages

Language learning has been my favorite hobby for over a decade now, starting with French and progressing to Spanish, Latin, Ancient Greek, German, Hindi, and most recently Chinese (to varying degrees of proficiency). In high school, I competed and won first place in both the North American Computational Linguistics Olympiad and the International Linguistics Olympiad.

Chinese

Ever since I started the Duolingo Chinese course in 2023, the Chinese language has been one of my life's great passions. In March 2026, I accomplished one of my longstanding goals by passing level 6 of the rigorous HSK exam, indicating "near-native proficiency," after three years of intensive self-study and regular online lessons. During the same time period, I have been fortunate enough to study Chinese calligraphy at Carnegie Mellon, dabble a bit in Classical Chinese, and travel to China, particularly the southwest, several times.

If you speak Chinese, are interested in learning, or want to practice, I would love to hear from you!

中文

自从2023年开始学习多邻国的中文课程以来，中文变成了我生活中极大的热忱所在。经过三年的努力自学与常规的在线课程， 我2026年3月终于实现了一个长期的目标：通过了汉语水平考试六级，这代表“接近母语者的熟练程度”。 在此期间，我有幸在卡内基梅隆大学学习了中国书法，涉猎了少量文言文知识，并几次去了中国旅行，尤其是西南地区。

如果你会说中文、有兴趣学习或者想练习，请随时联系我！

Ranch

Two childhood friends and I bought 10 acres of undeveloped land in southern Colorado, christening it Riddlers Ranch, and built a small cabin there entirely from scratch with no hired labor. Through this process, we learned a ton about everything from electrical wiring to stucco application to how to properly seal saltillo tile, mostly from YouTube videos. It was definitely more work than we bargained for originally, but worth it to be able to share with friends :)
