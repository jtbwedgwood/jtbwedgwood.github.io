Ongoing Projects

Conserved Quantities in Magnetohydrodynamics (with Barnabas Poczos)

The study of conserved quantities is central to many branches of physics, but ML-driven discovery of such quantities has largely focused on simple systems with few independent variables. My summer 2026 research aims to use ML techniques to find conserved quantities (like the infamous Hosking invariant) in the much richer setting of 3D magnetohydrodynamics, by applying methods from multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization, level-set estimation, and Bayesian quadrature.

Open-Reflection / Safety Nudges (with Chhavi Yadav and Virginia Smith)

The overwhelming majority of consumer interactions with LLMs take place through a small handful of online chatbot interfaces. Despite a recent surge of interest in AI safety interventions, these theoretical advancements are irrelevant to such users unless the companies that own those interfaces choose to implement them — which is, of course, hardly a guarantee. The Open-Reflection project aims to return agency to end-users by providing a Chrome extension (Safety Nudges) that screens chatbot conversations in real time for common pitfalls like flattery, overconfidence, and anthropomorphization, as well as associated datasets to help the community understand how these issues manifest in the real world.

Learn more about Open-Reflection or download Safety Nudges on the Chrome Web Store.
